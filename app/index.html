Welcome to AgentOX. From Jira ticket to reviewed PR. Autonomously.

Purpose of AgentOX

AgentOX is a web application for software teams. Its purpose is to take a Jira ticket through requirements, implementation, and QA, then open a draft pull request for human review. Sign in with Google to create or access your AgentOX workspace and connect tools you authorize, such as Jira, GitHub, or Bitbucket.
